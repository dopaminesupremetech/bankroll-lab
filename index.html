🧭 How to interpret results

Break-even win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + net profit payout). Staking progressions change volatility and drawdown; they do not create an edge. Use win-rate estimates supported by your own historical results. Simulations exclude taxes, fees, limits, and market movement unless reflected in the payout assumption.
